Meet the Woman Behind the Vision

A story of curves, courage, and quiet transformation.

Rosalie Mengot

Founder & Creative Director

My name is Rosalie Mengot. I am a mother, a wife, a student, and a woman who has walked through the quiet battles of self‑image. I was born and raised in Cameroon, Africa, where French is my first language. This is why the name of this brand, Beautés de Merveille, carries such deep meaning for me. In English, it translates to “Marvelous Beauties” a tribute to every woman whose beauty deserves to be honored, celebrated, and seen.

At 35, after giving birth, I experienced significant weight gain. My body changed, and with it, the way the world treated me changed too. Suddenly, finding clothing that fit my size and silhouette became a daily struggle. Stores that once felt welcoming now felt like reminders that fashion was not designed with women like me in mind.

I spent years trying to shrink myself emotionally, even as my body grew into a new shape. But one day, I realized something powerful: My body was not the problem. The problem was a world that had forgotten to make space for women like me.

Beautés de Merveille was born from that realization, a vision to create a digital sanctuary where curvy women feel seen, cherished, and beautifully represented. This space is my love letter to every woman who has ever felt overlooked, underestimated, or uncelebrated because of her size.

Here, your curves are not something to hide. They are something to honor. Something to dress with intention. Something to love without apology.

My hope is that Beautés de Merveille becomes more than a website, that it becomes a soft place to land, a reminder of your worth, and a celebration of the beauty that has always lived within you.
